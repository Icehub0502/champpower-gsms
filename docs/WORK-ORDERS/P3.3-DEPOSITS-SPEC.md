# P3.3 - Deposits (มัดจำ) - spec

Scope set by Ice via the coordinator (21 Oct 2026). Rules: CLAUDE.md s5.4 (1-10), s5.3, s5.8, s7 item 3b, s12.1.
Migration: `packages/db/migrations/0026_deposits.sql`. Code: `services/deposits.ts`, `routes/deposits.ts`,
`repositories/deposits.ts`, `packages/domain/src/rules/deposit.ts`, `packages/contracts/src/deposits.ts`.

## Stories and acceptance criteria

1. **Take a deposit for a customer.** `customer_id` required; job and vehicle optional.
   - AC: POST with an existing customer, a job (customer taken from the job), or name + phone (minimal customer, no car, no job).
   - AC: amount > 0; `expectedDate` mandatory and not in the past; channel (walk_in/page/line/phone), reason (booking/special_part/large_job) and one of the shop's payment channels mandatory. Field errors (400) come before any CHECK.
   - AC: a phone that matches one customer reuses that customer; 2-3 matches answer 409 `customer_ambiguous`; a blocklisted placeholder phone creates a new customer.
   - AC: the same `idempotencyKey` returns the same deposit (200, `replayed: true`); two concurrent calls make one deposit and one customer.
   - AC: optional slip image uploaded via `POST /api/files/deposit-slip`; read back only through `GET /api/files/:id` (shop-checked, technician refused).
2. **Never deleted; every leave from held is recorded.** AC: DB trigger blocks DELETE and any change to money columns; refund/forfeit store reason, who, when and are audited.
3. **Money is real on the day received.** AC: daily summary "เงินเข้า" includes deposits on the received day by channel/method, refunds on the refund day, never `deposit_offset`; sales never include deposits. The 2,000 (day 3) / 7,500 + 9,500 (day 8) example holds: day 3 money in 2,000, sales 0; day 8 money in 7,500, sales 9,500.
4. **Slip "ใบรับเงินมัดจำ".** AC: own `deposit_receipt` document type, DEP series; number allocated by the print action in the same transaction as the document row; title is the literal `ใบรับเงินมัดจำ`; shows the quotation number if linked at numbering; goes through `document_prints` (print count, changed-content warning, e.g. after a refund).
5. **Warning on job open.** AC: check-in and booking arrival responses carry `heldDeposits` (amount, received date, channel, expected date, overdue, matched by customer/booking/phone). `GET /api/jobs/:jobId/deposits` and the receipt view (`deposits.available` / `deposits.unattached`) expose it. `POST /api/deposits/:id/attach {jobId}` binds it. Technicians never get amounts.
6. **Offset into the bill.** AC: `POST .../receipt/close` takes `depositIds`; one `deposit_offset` payment row per deposit with `deposit_id`; deposit becomes `applied` in the same transaction; `grand_total` unchanged; no VAT computed; several deposits per job; `deposit_held` blocker removed; receipt shows each offset with its DEP number; deposits above the bill are refused (`deposit_exceeds_bill`); deposits covering the whole bill need no payments.
7. **"มัดจำค้างอยู่".** AC: list held deposits with customer, amount, since, channel, plus total held and overdue count/total; overdue the day after `expectedDate`. Refund: advisor and above, reason required. Forfeit: manager and above, reason required, shows in the daily summary as "รายได้จากมัดจำที่ริบ" (not sales, no VAT). Nothing is automatic.
8. **Concurrency/lock order.** jobs -> documents -> lines -> deposits. A refund racing a close: close re-reads after the lock and answers 409 `deposit_not_held`.

## Defaults (marked `DEFAULT pending Ice (P3.3 Dn)` in code)

- D1 The "print slip" action allocates the DEP number (not row creation).
- D2 A refund goes out through the channel the money came in through unless another is named.
- D3 "Manager and above" for forfeit = owner + manager (assistant_manager cannot forfeit).
- D4 Forfeit is allowed before the deposit is overdue (reason is what records why).
- D5 `expectedDate` may be today but not earlier.
- D6 A deposit is offset in full or not at all (no partial application).
- D7 Voiding a receipt returns the deposits it consumed to `held` (owner action, audited).
- D8 No channel surcharge on deposits.
- D9 Blocklisted phone -> new customer; `lead_source` not filled from the deposit channel.
- D10 The quotation number is frozen into the slip when it is first numbered (a later attach does not change a numbered slip).
- D11 `expected_date` cannot be changed after the deposit is taken (no reschedule endpoint).
- D12 Roles: take/attach/detach/print/refund = owner, manager, assistant_manager, advisor; read = those + accountant, viewer; technician sees nothing.

## Open questions for Ice

1. Should a customer who postpones be able to move `expectedDate` (reschedule with a reason)? Not built.
2. Should an assistant_manager be allowed to forfeit (D3)?
3. If deposits exceed the bill, should part of a deposit be applied and the rest kept (D6), or keep refusing?
4. Voiding a receipt: return to `held` (D7) or require a refund decision?
5. Card/QR fee on a deposit paid by card (D8)?
6. Should the slip be reprinted with the quotation number once a job is attached (needs a new numbered slip or a revision)?
7. Forfeit income without VAT is the owner's decision, not a tax conclusion - confirm with the accountant when totals grow.
